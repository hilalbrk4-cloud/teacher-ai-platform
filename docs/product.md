# EduPilot Product Specification

## Product Summary

EduPilot is an AI-powered workspace created for teachers. Its purpose is to help teachers spend less time preparing materials and more time teaching.

EduPilot is not an LMS, school administration panel, student portal, or parent portal. Version 1 focuses only on teachers.

## Primary Users

- Primary school teachers
- Middle school teachers
- High school teachers

The initial target market is Türkiye.

## Product Promise

EduPilot should help a teacher create high-quality educational materials quickly, consistently, and with less effort.

Its difference from a general-purpose chatbot is:

- teacher-specific guided workflows
- structured educational outputs
- natural Turkish terminology
- reusable documents
- editable results
- export and saving workflows
- consistent pedagogical quality
- less prompt-writing effort

## Product Personality

EduPilot should feel intelligent, trustworthy, calm, modern, supportive, professional, and efficient.

It should never feel childish, noisy, overly decorative, or robotic.

## Language

Turkish is the default interface language.

Visible UI copy must sound natural, use terminology familiar to teachers in Türkiye, avoid literal machine translations, and remain short and clear.

## Version 1 Scope

- Dashboard
- AI Tools
- Lesson Planner
- Quiz Generator
- Worksheet Generator
- Rubric Generator
- Presentation Builder
- Text Simplifier
- Documents
- Favorites
- History
- Settings

Not included:

- student accounts
- parent accounts
- school administration
- grading system
- attendance system
- full LMS functionality

## AI Philosophy

AI should not replace the teacher. It should help the teacher begin faster, structure ideas, adapt content, save time, revise outputs, and export usable documents.

The teacher must remain in control of the final result.
