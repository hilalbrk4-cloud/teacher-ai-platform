# EduPilot UX Principles

## Teacher-First

Reduce teacher effort. Do not require teachers to understand prompt engineering.

## AI-First, Not Chat-Only

Use guided inputs, smart defaults, examples, structured outputs, and editing tools. Do not turn every feature into a generic chat box.

## Fast to Start

Users should understand what to do within seconds.

## Progressive Disclosure

Show essential fields first. Place advanced controls in expandable sections where appropriate.

## Teacher Control

AI output must be editable, regeneratable, savable, copyable, and exportable where relevant.

## Clear System Status

Always communicate loading, success, error, saved state, unsaved changes, and generation progress.

## Natural Turkish

Use language teachers in Türkiye naturally understand.

## Prevent Errors

Use validation, sensible defaults, confirmation for destructive actions, and recoverable states.

## Consistency

Navigation, buttons, forms, cards, and messages must behave consistently across features.

## Accessibility

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, meaningful labels, readable text sizes, and semantic elements.

## Empty States

Explain what the section is, why it is useful, and what the user should do next.

## AI Output Experience

Generated content should be structured and easy to scan. Provide edit, copy, save, export, regenerate, and refine actions where relevant.
